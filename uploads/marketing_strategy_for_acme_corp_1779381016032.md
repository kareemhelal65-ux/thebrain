# Marketing Strategy for ACME Corp
## Executive Summary
ACME Corp aims to increase brand awareness and drive sales through a multi-channel approach.
## Target Audience
* Demographics: Ages 25-45
* Interests: Technology, innovation
## Marketing Channels
* Social Media: LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook
* Content Marketing: Blog posts, whitepapers, case studies
## Budget Allocation
* Social Media: 30%
* Content Marketing: 40%
* Paid Advertising: 30%
## Timeline
* Month 1-3: Establish social media presence, launch content marketing campaign
* Month 4-6: Optimize and refine marketing strategy based on performance data
