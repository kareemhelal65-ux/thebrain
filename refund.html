1. 14-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee for all initial subscription purchases (including our $39/month Founder Edition plan) and recurring renewals. If you decide that Brain aOS is not a fit for your workflow, you can request a full refund within fourteen (14) days of the transaction date.

Refund requests submitted after the 14-day window are generally not eligible for refunds. However, you can cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future automated billing.

2. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

To request a refund, please choose one of the following methods:

  • Email Support: Send an email from your registered account address to kareem@onethirty-eg.com with the subject line "Refund Request". Please include your transaction receipt or Paddle Order ID.
  • Paddle Customer Portal: You can manage your subscription and request refunds directly via the Paddle billing link provided in your email invoice.

3. PROCESSING AND TIMELINE

Once we receive and verify your refund request:

  • We will process your refund immediately.
  • Your subscription will be terminated, and your account access will revert to our free draft level or be deactivated.
  • The refunded amount will be credited back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or credit card provider, the funds typically appear in your account within 5 to 10 business days.

4. PADDLE TRANSACTIONS

Because Paddle is our Merchant of Record, your credit card statement or bank logs will show transactions labeled as PADDLE.NET * BRAIN AOS or similar. Paddle handles tax calculation, local currency conversion, and fraud checks on our behalf.

5. DISPUTES AND CHARGEBACKS

We ask that you contact us directly at kareem@onethirty-eg.com before filing a dispute or chargeback with your financial institution. Disputes can take several weeks to resolve. We aim to process all valid refund requests within 24 to 48 hours, which is significantly faster and easier for both parties.

6. POLICY ABUSE AND EXCLUSIONS

To protect the integrity of our platform, we reserve the right to deny refund requests if we detect clear signs of policy abuse. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Repeatedly signing up and requesting refunds in consecutive billing periods.
  • Accounts terminated due to violations of our Terms of Service (e.g. data scraping or illegal utilization of AI agents).
