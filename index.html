General Inquiries

Is this just another meeting summarizer?

No. Meeting summarizers write notes. Brain aOS ingests your entire corporate knowledge graph (docs, CSVs, Slack, transcripts), resolves dependencies, and delegates actual tasks to specialized agents (e.g. drafting waitlist campaigns, calculating burns, preparing Fast agreements)—all staged for your final approval.

How is client data protected?

We deploy database-level Row Level Security (RLS) and separate vector namespaces per tenant. Furthermore, the Sentinel pipeline automatically identifies and redacts personally identifiable information (PII) before queries leave the brain for external model API routing.

How does the Human-in-the-Loop policy function?

By default, agents operate in planning mode. They output structured strategies or preview-ready deliverables. The human must review, provide revision comments, or approve the outcome. Brain aOS will never execute write operations to external services without your sign-off.

Is the platform really free?

It is free only through the beta phase to a limited number of people that join the waitlist.

Where is Brain aOS built?

Cairo, Egypt. 🇪🇬 We build robust, structured cognitive software outside of Silicon Valley, serving founders worldwide.
