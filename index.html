// 01 THE PROBLEM

Your Company's Knowledge Is Scattered

Decisions Get Lost

Every meeting generates critical decisions that nobody writes down. Decisions vanish into Slack threads or thin air within 48 hours.

Tasks Slip Through

Action items are verbalized during syncs or calls but never captured in a central tracker. Teams waste hours daily recovering context.

Single Point of Failure

Institutional knowledge is locked in individual brains. When a teammate goes on leave or departs, weeks of operational context disappear with them.

// 02 THE SOLUTION

Fireflies Transcribes. Brain aOS Acts.

Persistent Memory

Brain aOS unifies transcripts, documents, email, and files in a secure semantic memory. Ask anything in natural language and get instant answers with source citations.

Agent Delegation

Brain aOS delegates tasks to specialized AI agents—Finance, HR, Sales, Marketing, PM. Brain aOS creates strategies, tracks objectives, and does real work.

Human-in-the-Loop

No emails, roadmap updates, or transactions are executed autonomously without review. The AI drafts and packages deliverables; you approve or request revisions.

Download the platform and run the entire stack offline via local AI models (such as Llama 3 or Ollama) to keep your corporate intelligence completely private.

Telegram Control Center

Auto-extract contacts from files and meetings. Profiles are ranked by AI importance scores, displaying a 360-degree timeline of linked documents, tasks, and meetings.

Sales & Investor Pipelines

Hybrid Metrics & Analytics

Track department health with live integration metrics and manual overrides. Analytical sweeps scan metrics and central memory to produce deep AI insights.

Semantic Goal Deduplication

Our semantic router runs clustering algorithms across your active North Star goals, automatically merging equivalents while preserving target metrics and descriptions.

Brain aOS ingests your entire corporate knowledge graph (docs, CSVs, Slack, transcripts), resolves dependencies, and delegates actual tasks to specialized agents (e.g. drafting waitlist campaigns, calculating burns, preparing Fast agreements)—all staged for your final approval.

We deploy database-level Row Level Security (RLS) and separate vector namespaces per tenant. Furthermore, the Sentinel pipeline automatically identifies and redacts personally identifiable information (PII) before queries leave the brain for external model API routing.

By default, agents operate in planning mode. They output structured strategies or preview-ready deliverables. The human must review, provide revision comments, or approve the outcome. Brain aOS will never execute write operations to external services without your sign-off.
