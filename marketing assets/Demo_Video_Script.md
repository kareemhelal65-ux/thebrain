# Asset 6: Demo Video Script — "The 60-Second Demo"

**Instructions:** Record this using OBS (screen recording) + your phone or a USB microphone for voiceover. Total runtime: 60-75 seconds. The "holy shit" moment is at the 30-second mark. That's what makes people share it.

**Equipment needed:**
- OBS Studio (free) for screen recording
- Any microphone (phone mic works in a pinch)
- Brain aOS dashboard open and ready
- Pre-loaded sample meeting data (or real data if you have it)

---

## PRE-RECORDING SETUP

Before you hit record:

1. Open Brain aOS dashboard in full-screen mode
2. Have a sample meeting already ingested (or use a demo account with sample data)
3. Close all browser tabs except Brain aOS
4. Clear your desktop of clutter
5. Set display resolution to 1920x1080
6. Open a blank question in the chat interface — cursor ready to type
7. Close Slack, email, everything that might pop up notifications
8. Do one practice run without recording

---

## POST-PRODUCTION

### Editing Checklist (Use CapCut or DaVinci Resolve — both free):

1. **Trim** to exactly 60-75 seconds
2. **Add captions** (auto-generate in CapCut, then proofread):
   - Captions should appear word-by-word as you speak
   - Highlight key phrases in your brand orange (#FF5A1F)
3. **Add subtle background music:**
   - Low-energy lo-fi or ambient track (YouTube Audio Library, search "corporate ambient" or "tech background")
   - Volume: 10-15% — voice should dominate
4. **Smooth transitions** between screens:
   - Quick cuts only. No fancy transitions. This is a product demo, not a movie.
5. **Zoom + pan** on key moments:
   - Slight zoom when the answer appears
   - Slight zoom on the "Approve" button click
   - Slight zoom on the "citation/source card"
6. **Title card** at the end (3 seconds):
   - Brain aOS logo
   - "Beta Open — Free"
   - Link URL visible
7. **Export settings:**
   - 1080p, 30fps (or 60fps if motion is important)
   - H.264 codec
   - File size target: under 50MB for fast uploading

## Variations to Record After the Main Video

Once you have the main 60-second version working, record these variations:

### Variation A: "Meeting Summary" (45 seconds)
- Connect Google → meeting auto-ingested → show the meeting summary card with decisions + action items
- Hook: "Your meeting ended 2 minutes ago. The AI already summarized it."

### Variation B: "Agent Delegation" (60 seconds)
- Full flow: Ask question → Brain delegates to Finance Agent → Finance Agent analyzes → returns results
- Hook: "I didn't ask ChatGPT. I asked my Finance Agent."

### Variation C: "The Rollback" (45 seconds)
- AI drafts email → founder clicks Undo → email is recalled
- Hook: "AI made a mistake. I fixed it in one click."

### Variation D: "The Contrast" (60 seconds)
- Side by side: Fireflies output vs. Brain aOS output for the same meeting
- Hook: "Fireflies gives you a transcript. Brain aOS gives you action items, a follow-up email, and a decision log."

### Variation E: "Founder Testimonial" (60 seconds)
- Record yourself (face cam + screen) talking about why you built this
- More personal, more emotional
- Hook: "I'm building an AI operating system from Cairo, Egypt. Here's why."
