# Cortex OS — Banner Typography Guide

The typography used in the official high-resolution `banner.png` (4800x2520) is defined as follows:

## Primary Title ("Cortex OS")
- **Font Family:** Arial, sans-serif
- **Font Size:** 160px
- **Font Weight:** Bold
- **Color:** `#000000` (Black)
- **Letter Spacing:** -4px
- **Styling Note:** Tightly tracked and heavy to create a dominant, foundational aesthetic.

## Subtitle ("INTELLIGENCE BUILT ON STRUCTURE")
- **Font Family:** Courier New, monospace
- **Font Size:** 34px
- **Font Weight:** Bold
- **Color:** `#4b5563` (Dark Gray / Gray-600)
- **Letter Spacing:** 8px
- **Styling Note:** Wide tracking with a monospace technical font to juxtapose the dense main title and evoke a structural, computational feel.
