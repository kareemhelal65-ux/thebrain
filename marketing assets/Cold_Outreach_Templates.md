# Asset 4: Cold Outreach DM Templates — 50 Templates (5 Categories x 10 Each)

**Instructions:** Each template is a starting point. PERSONALIZE every DM with the recipient's name, company, and a specific detail from their profile/posts. Generic DMs get ignored. Personalized DMs get replies.

Send 10-20 DMs per day. Track who replies. Follow up in 3 days if no response.

**Rules:**
- NEVER send the template as-is. Always personalize the first line.
- Keep it under 3 sentences before the CTA.
- Never pitch. Offer value or curiosity.
- If they don't reply, ONE follow-up max. Then move on.

**Daily targets:**
- 10-20 new DMs sent
- 5-10 follow-ups sent
- Track reply rate (aim for 10-15%)
- Track conversion to waitlist signup (aim for 3-5% of those who reply)

**What to do when someone replies positively:**
1. Thank them
2. Send the demo video link
3. Offer a live walkthrough call
4. Ask them to share with one person who might find it useful
5. Add them to your "early advocates" list for Product Hunt upvotes
